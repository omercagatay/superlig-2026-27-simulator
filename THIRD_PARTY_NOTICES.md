## openfootball football.json

`data/superlig_results.csv` also includes the 27 2024-25 Süper Lig matches of
matchdays 10–12 (25 October – 10 November 2024), which the archive above
lacks, taken from [openfootball/football.json](https://github.com/openfootball/football.json),
file [`2024-25/tr.1.json`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openfootball/football.json/master/2024-25/tr.1.json)
(retrieved 2026-10-19, SHA-256
`6bfa0c16f87ea59ab79c60873ad3d3c0288e3198e4d6d0f56a2ebcb117e708c4`).
